About OrthoPlus

Bone and joint care, start to finish

OrthoPlus brings orthopedic surgeons, imaging and physiotherapy under one roof. Most patients never need surgery: we start with the right diagnosis, targeted rehab and injections, and only recommend an operation when it will truly help.

  • Same-day X-ray and fast MRI access on site
  • Non-surgical treatment first, including PRP and guided injections
  • Robotic-assisted and arthroscopic (keyhole) surgery
  • In-house physiotherapists for a seamless recovery
