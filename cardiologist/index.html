About HeartWise

Prevention first, precision always

HeartWise is an independent cardiology practice focused on finding heart problems early and treating them with the latest evidence. From your first ECG to long-term care, one dedicated team looks after your heart and keeps your family doctor informed.

  • Full diagnostic suite under one roof, no running between labs
  • Clear explanations and a written plan after every visit
  • Fast referrals to top hospitals when procedures are needed
  • Remote monitoring for blood pressure and heart rhythm
