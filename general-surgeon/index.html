About SurgiCare

Surgery you can feel calm about

SurgiCare is a surgeon-led practice for common general surgery procedures. We take the time to explain every option, including when surgery is not needed, and use minimally invasive keyhole techniques whenever possible for less pain and a quicker return to normal life.

  • One surgeon from first consultation to final follow-up
  • Keyhole (laparoscopic) surgery for most procedures
  • Operating at accredited partner hospitals and surgery centers
  • 24/7 post-op helpline answered by our surgical team

What will my recovery look like?

Choose a procedure to see typical recovery milestones after keyhole surgery. Everyone heals differently, and your surgeon will give you a personal plan.

After surgery, call us or 911 if you have:

  • Fever above 101°F (38.3°C)
  • Increasing pain, redness or discharge from a wound
  • Vomiting or unable to keep fluids down
  • Shortness of breath or calf pain and swelling
